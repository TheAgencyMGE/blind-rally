Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

Short version: Blind Rally has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no cookies and no server of its own that receives your data. Your stats live in your browser. Your location is used only while you play, only in the foreground, and is never stored.

Location

  • We ask for location permission only when you tap Use my location, or when a rally starts and needs to detect your arrival.
  • Your position is checked only while the game is open and visible. When you switch apps or lock your phone, GPS checking stops. There is no background tracking.
  • Your track is never recorded. The game keeps a running distance total in memory and discards each position after using it.
  • To find a destination and a route, your starting coordinates are sent to the public OpenStreetMap services listed below. Nothing identifies you in those requests beyond what any web request reveals (such as your IP address).
  • You can always pick a starting point on the map instead of sharing your location.

What stays on your device

Stored in your browser's local storage, and nowhere else:

  • Settings (mode, difficulty, units, distance, vibration, screen-awake).
  • Stats: completed and abandoned counts, best times, your last 30 rallies (destination name, time, difficulty, date) and identifiers of destinations you've discovered so you aren't sent to the same place twice.
  • While a rally is in progress: the hidden destination, so you can recover from an accidental refresh. It is deleted the moment the rally ends.

Starting locations are never stored. You can erase everything in Settings → Erase all local data, or by clearing site data in your browser.

Hosting

The site is served as static files. The host (for example Cloudflare Pages) may keep standard request logs; Blind Rally adds no tracking of its own.

Sharing

The Share button uses your device's share sheet. The shared text contains your time, difficulty and route length — never coordinates or the destination's name.

Safety

Play responsibly. In driving mode, a passenger must operate the phone at all times. Obey traffic laws, respect private property and stay aware of your surroundings.

Contact
